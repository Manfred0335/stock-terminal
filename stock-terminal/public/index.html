      Projected price

      CAGR path compounds the historical total-return rate (price growth + dividend) forward. Monte Carlo resamples the stock's real daily moves (fat tails, not a bell curve) with the growth rate shrunk halfway toward a ~7% market average so a recent hot streak doesn't extrapolate to the moon — the band shows the 10th–90th percentile range. Still a model, not a forecast; real returns depend on fundamentals and conditions that change.

      Scoring Framework

      METHODOLOGY

      Momentum · 18%

      3–6 month returns, price vs 50/200-day averages, RSI & MACD trend.

      Fundamentals · 20%

      Net & gross margins, return on equity, revenue growth.

      Valuation · 17%

      P/E, PEG, price-to-book and analyst target upside.

      Catalysts · 13%

      Upcoming earnings, analyst rating trend, news sentiment, target upside.

      Risk · 17%

      Beta, annualized volatility, drawdown, size. Higher = safer.

      Cashflow · 15%

      Free-cash-flow yield, FCF-per-share vs EPS conversion, FCF margin & trend.

      The composite is a weighted blend of whichever factors have data (weights re-normalize when a factor is missing). Factors tagged proxy use the 52-week range when full price history isn't loaded; est cashflow is inferred from margins when statements aren't available. This is a quantitative screen, not investment advice.
